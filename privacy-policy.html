1. Information We Collect

When you browse crumblyblr.shop, configure cookie boxes, or place an order, we may collect the following information:

  • Recipient name and contact telephone number
  • Delivery address, pincode, state, and city
  • Email address (for invoice receipt and live tracking notifications)
  • Purchased items, pack weights, and order timestamps
  • Technical information such as device type, browser, and generalized traffic parameters to ensure optimal site rendering

2. How We Use Your Information

Your details are used exclusively for legitimate business purposes:

  • Baking, packaging, and dispatching your cookie orders
  • Transmitting automated dispatch updates, tracking links, and delivery confirmations
  • Providing responsive support via WhatsApp and email
  • Preventing fraudulent transactions and ensuring billing integrity

3. Payment Security & Shopify Processing

All financial transactions are conducted directly via Shopify’s PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant checkout infrastructure. Crumbly never accesses, processes, or stores your complete credit/debit card numbers, CVV codes, or UPI PINs on its own servers.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

We use lightweight browser cookies and local storage exclusively to remember your selected items, cart quantities, and recently tracked AWB numbers so you do not need to re-type them on return visits.

5. Sharing of Information

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We only share minimal necessary shipping data with certified courier partners (such as Shiprocket, Xpressbees, or Delhivery) solely to deliver your parcel to your address.

6. Data Security

We apply 256-bit SSL encryption across all website interactions, ensuring your personal details remain confidential and protected from unauthorized access.

7. Your Privacy Rights

You have full rights to request an export of data held in connection with your previous orders or request the deletion of non-accounting contact data by emailing our team.

8. Third-Party Links

Our website may include links to Instagram, WhatsApp, or courier tracking portals. Each third party operates under its respective privacy terms.

9. Children’s Privacy

Our store is intended for general audiences. We do not intentionally harvest data from individuals under 18 years of age without parental supervision.

10. Policy Updates

Any revisions to our privacy protocols will be reflected transparently on this page with an updated timestamp.
