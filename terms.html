1. About Crumbly

Crumbly is a specialty bakery brand offering gourmet butter coin cookies through its direct-to-consumer store. We offer three distinct flavours: Double Chocolate, Choco Chips, and Red Velvet, baked with 100% pure butter.

2. Product Information

We take extreme care to accurately portray cookie visuals, net weights, and nutritional profiles. Slight natural variations in crust texture or chocolate chip distribution may occur between individual artisan batches.

3. Product Availability

All items are freshly baked and subject to daily batch limits. In the rare event an ordered item cannot be dispatched within standard timelines, our support team will promptly communicate options for immediate dispatch or full refund.

4. Pricing & Taxes

All listed prices are denominated in Indian Rupees (₹) and are inclusive of all applicable statutory GST. Shipping fees, if any, are clearly stated prior to final payment authorization.

5. Orders & Information Accuracy

Customers must provide accurate contact and delivery information at checkout. Crumbly is not responsible for failed deliveries resulting from incomplete postal addresses or incorrect contact numbers.

6. Payment Terms

We operate on a 100% prepaid basis (via UPI, credit/debit cards, and net banking). Cash on Delivery (COD) is not supported to guarantee that freshly baked food packages are received without delay.

7. Cancellation Policy

Because Crumbly cookies are baked and sealed specifically for each order, cancellation is not possible after payment completion. Please review your order carefully before checking out.

8. Delivery Terms

Orders are dispatched within 1–2 business days and standard pan-India transit time is 7–8 days by surface transport. Deliveries are subject to courier network operating conditions.
