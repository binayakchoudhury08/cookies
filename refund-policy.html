1. Order Cancellation

Orders cannot be cancelled once payment has been successfully completed. Before completing payment, please carefully verify:

  • Product selection (Double Chocolate, Choco Chips, or Red Velvet)
  • Box format (Mini 80g, Single 180g, or Duo 360g)
  • Quantity desired
  • Full delivery address including flat/house number and pincode
  • Phone number and email address for dispatch notifications

Once payment is confirmed, your order enters our immediate baking and small-batch packaging queue. This policy is subject to applicable law and does not limit any consumer rights that cannot legally be excluded.

2. Returns

Because Crumbly products are perishable food items baked with 100% pure dairy butter, we cannot accept returns of correctly delivered food packages. We cannot accept returns or provide refunds for:

  • Change of mind after shipment
  • Ordering an unintended flavour
  • Ordering an unintended quantity
  • Incorrect delivery address provided during checkout
  • Unavailability or inability to receive the parcel at the designated destination

3. Damaged Products (48-Hour Guarantee)

If your Crumbly order arrives visibly damaged or tampered during transport, please notify us within 48 hours of package delivery. Please share:

  • Order number or AWB tracking ID
  • Clear photographs showing the outer transit box and shipping label
  • Clear photographs of the damaged inner cookie tins or coins
  • A brief description of what happened

Our customer support team will immediately review the proof and arrange an expedited replacement batch or a full refund.

4. Incorrect Products

If you receive a flavour or box size that does not match your confirmed order invoice, WhatsApp us within 48 hours with a photograph of the received item and your order number. Following swift verification, we will immediately dispatch the correct product or process a full refund.

5. Missing Items

If any item or add-on is missing from your parcel, inform us within 48 hours. We cross-verify packing weights recorded at dispatch and will immediately dispatch the missing items via express courier or credit the difference.

6. Refunds & Timelines

Where a refund is approved by our team, it is credited back to your original payment method (UPI, credit/debit card, or net banking via Shopify Payments). Refunds reach your account within 5–7 business days depending on your banking provider’s settlement cycle.

7. Refund Exclusions

Refunds will not be granted for orders where delivery failed due to incorrect address information, repeated customer unavailability, refusal of package acceptance, or where items have been opened and consumed past verification inspection.

8. Quality Concerns

We pride ourselves on our 100% pure butter formulation. If you ever perceive an unexpected taste or seal defect, contact us immediately with batch timestamps printed on your box lid.
