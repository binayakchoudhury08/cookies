1. Order Processing & Baking

Once your order has been successfully placed and payment confirmed, our bakery begins batching and packaging your cookie coins. Orders typically complete packing and carrier handover within 1–2 business days.

2. Delivery Timeline

Our standard estimated delivery timeline is 7–8 days across India by surface transport from the date of order confirmation.

Transit timelines may vary based on:

  • Metro vs non-metro regional destinations
  • Courier operational loads and airline capacity
  • Severe weather conditions or local transit advisories
  • National and state holidays

3. Tracking Your Order

Your unique AWB tracking ID arrives by email and WhatsApp as soon as your box leaves our facility. You can monitor your shipment directly on our Live Track Order Page anytime.

4. Delivery Address Accuracy

Please ensure that your delivery address, flat/house number, landmark, pin code, and recipient telephone number are entered completely and accurately before completing checkout. Crumbly is not responsible for failed deliveries arising from incorrect or incomplete postal credentials.

5. Delayed Deliveries

While over 94% of our orders arrive right on or before the 7–8 day window, occasional carrier delays can occur during high festive seasons. If your delivery exceeds this timeline, reach out with your AWB number and we will actively intervene with the courier team.

6. Damaged Shipments (48-Hour Reporting)

If your package arrives crushed or open, report damage within 48 hours of delivery. Retain all original packaging material until our team has reviewed the claim. Replacements or refunds are settled within 5–7 business days.

7. Incorrect or Missing Products

If your delivered parcel does not match your purchase invoice, please notify us within 48 hours. Upon rapid digital verification, we will dispatch the missing items or issue a full credit immediately.

8. Shipping Charges

Applicable shipping and handling fees are calculated based on destination pincode and displayed transparently during checkout before final payment.

9. Address Modifications

Because orders enter automated logistics staging shortly after payment, shipping address alterations are only possible if requested before courier dispatch. Contact us on WhatsApp immediately if you need an urgent modification.
