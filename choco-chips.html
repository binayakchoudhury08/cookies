About Choco Chips

Our signature Choco Chips coin cookies bring the quintessential butter cookie back to its artisanal origins. Hand-poured with dark chocolate chips that melt softly onto the palate, backed by real pure churned creamery dairy butter. Clean, bite-sized coins engineered for zero crumb mess.

Ingredients: 100% Pure Dairy Butter, Refined Wheat Flour, Premium Chocolate Chips (Cocoa Mass, Sugar, Cocoa Butter), Unrefined Cane Sugar, Brown Sugar, Pure Bourbon Vanilla Extract, Sea Salt, Raising Agent (INS 500ii).

Allergen Declaration: Contains Milk (Pure Butter) & Wheat (Gluten). Baked in a 100% vegetarian, eggless bakery.

Keep the box sealed in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Enjoy within 30 days of baking for optimal crispness and gooey center.

For an oven-fresh gooey experience, microwave 2–3 coins for 5 to 8 seconds, or warm them in a preheated oven at 140°C for 90 seconds. The chocolate chips will turn warm and silky!

Baked in small batches in Bengaluru and shipped pan-India by surface courier in 7–8 days. Your tracking ID arrives by email and WhatsApp once the box ships. If your box arrives damaged, WhatsApp us photos within 48 hours and we will replace it or refund you.
